<h2>What it is</h2>
<p>Vitamin D is not one molecule but a small family of fat-soluble compounds. The word “vitamin” fits here: this is an organic molecule, not a mineral element.</p>

<h2>Why it comes up with calcium</h2>
<p>NIH ODS notes that vitamin D is required for calcium to be absorbed in the gut by active transport and to maintain adequate calcium levels in the blood. Calcium itself is a mineral — a different category.</p>

<h2>What we don't say</h2>
<p>No amount, no “take this”, no promise. Norms belong to NIH/EFSA/WHO and are quoted with a date and a link.</p>
