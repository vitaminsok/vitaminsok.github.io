Method

Vita is a daily, evidence-first note about micronutrients — vitamins and mineral elements. Every number comes from a public source; nothing here is a recommendation.

Data sources

  • NIH Office of Dietary Supplements — fact sheets for vitamins and minerals.
  • EFSA and WHO — reference values and frameworks, cited with dates.
  • Europe PMC / PubMed eutils — recent studies with title, date and link.
  • Open Food Facts / USDA — food composition context, never a prescription.

Two categories, not one

Vitamins are organic molecules; calcium, magnesium, iron and zinc are inorganic mineral elements. Both are micronutrients, but the word “vitamin” does not cover minerals.

Hard rules

  • Informational and educational only — not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
  • Norms and amounts are quoted from NIH/EFSA/WHO with a source and date; no dose from us.
  • No promises of health, no supplement sales, no partner links, no personal health data.
  • A disclaimer appears on every public page.
